How to issue or redeem a card
  1. Take payment

    In the cart, select the owner and open the Gift Certificate line item. Enter the amount and collect payment.

  2. Prepare and record the card

    Write the amount and sign the card with a Sharpie. Log the certificate number and purchase information below. Put the card in an envelope for the customer.

  3. Record redemption

    Verify the certificate number and amount here, including any partial-use notes. Choose Gift Certificate as the payment method at checkout and fill in the redemption information below. Discard the physical card only once it is fully used.
